# Crops

Crops are the core of Croparia IF’s gameplay. They use farming as a framework to make a wide range of resource items farmable in bulk.

<a id='crop'></a>

## Fruit Crops

Fruit crops are similar to vanilla single-block crops, but they are mainly used to produce item materials. Every fruit crop has its own **Seeds**, **Fruit**, and **Crop Block**.

**Fruit** can be turned into item materials by right-clicking on the ground, through crafting, or by using the [Crop Transmuter](../blocks-and-items/workstations.md#croparia:crop_transmuter).

<a id='melon'></a>

## Melons

Melons are similar to vanilla vine crops such as pumpkins and melons, but they are mainly used to produce block materials. Every melon crop has its own **Melon Seeds**, **Melon**, and **Melon Stem**.

**Melon Stems** can be accelerated with Bone Meal, and using Bone Meal on a mature stem helps it produce fruit.

**Melons** can be turned into block materials through crafting or by using the [Crop Transmuter](../blocks-and-items/workstations.md#croparia:crop_transmuter).
