# Core Concepts

## Entry points

- [Crops](crop.md): explains how fruit crops and melons are structured, what they produce, and their baseline differences.
- [Elements](element.md): explains Elemental Gems, Elemental Potions, Elemental Fluids, and their basic roles across the system.
