# Blocks and Items

This group of pages organizes the mod’s explicitly visible blocks and items by entry type, making it easier to look up names, uses, and relationships.

## Entry points

- [Croparia](croparia.md): covers the roles, upgrade paths, and common crafting entry points of each Croparia tier.
- [Workstations](workstations.md): covers the Greenhouse, Infusor, Elemental Stone, Crop Transmuter, and Ritual Stand.
- [Relics](relic.md): covers special items such as the Horn of Plenty, Midas Hand, and Infinite Apple.
- [Other](others.md): collects items such as Elematilius Ore, Activated Shrieker, and the Recipe Wizard that do not fall into the categories above.
