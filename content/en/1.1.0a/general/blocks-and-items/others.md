# Other

<a id='elematilius_ore'></a>

## Elematilius Ore

<RowGallery>
<GameItemCard id="croparia:elematilius_ore"></GameItemCard>
<GameItemCard id="croparia:deepslate_elematilius_ore"></GameItemCard>
</RowGallery>

The original source of Elemental Gems. It generates at `y = 80` and below, and in theory becomes more common at lower heights. Mining it drops 1 to 2 [Elemental Gems](../concepts/element.md#gems), affected by Fortune and Silk Touch.

## Recipe Wizard

<div class="doc-center">
<GameItemCard id="croparia:recipe_wizard"></GameItemCard>
</div>

A creative-mode debugging tool used to generate recipe files more conveniently. It mainly serves systems such as the [Infusor](./workstations.md#croparia:infusor), [Elemental Stone](./workstations.md#croparia:elemental_stone), and [Ritual Stand](./workstations.md#croparia:ritual_stand). For detailed usage, see [Recipe Wizard (Modpack Authors)](../../modpack/recipe-wizard/index.md).
