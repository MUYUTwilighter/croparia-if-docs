# Modpack Authors

This section is for modpack authors who need to customize the behavior and content of Croparia IF. It is designed so you can perform many kinds of pack-side modifications without reading the source code.

Files created by users on top of Croparia IF, including pack-side customization content, belong entirely to their authors and do not require additional copyright permission from the mod author.
